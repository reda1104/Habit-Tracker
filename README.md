# Habit Tracker

A cross-platform habit tracking app built with Flutter.  
Track your daily habits, visualize your progress, and gain insights into your routines.

## Features

- 📅 **Track Habits:** Add, complete, and manage your daily habits.
- 📊 **Statistics:** Visualize your weekly activity with beautiful bar charts.
- 🔥 **Streaks:** See your longest streaks and stay motivated.
- 💡 **Insights:** Discover your most productive days.
- 🌙 **Dark Theme:** Modern, dark-themed UI for comfortable use.
